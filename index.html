01How it works

Three things happen on every run.

You run your normal route, or go looking. The phone stays in your pocket until you reach a rune.

1
󰜎

Start a run

Tap Start Run and go. GPS tracks your distance, time and pace. Ten runes appear on real streets around you, 200 to 650 metres away, in every direction.

2
󰋋

Reach a rune

Pick one and run to it. Within about 40 metres it lights up. Tap it, and the narrator starts the scene in your earbuds: a traveller, an ambush, a hidden cache.

3
󰝁

Choose what you do

Four big buttons, easy to hit mid-stride. A hidden dice roll decides how well it goes. Win gold, find gear, lose health, or start a fight. Then run on.

Hunt for runes and you find one about every 300 m. On a fixed route you pass one about every 650 m. Either way, a 5 km run is a handful of scenes.

You find out what it is when you get there.

02The game

A real character, built one run at a time.

Your character keeps everything between runs: level, gold, gear and health. Here is what there is to do.

󱅕

Fights with real dice

Combat uses the D&D 5e rules. Roll a d20, add your strength, and beat the enemy's armour to hit. Attack, use an ability, or try to run away.

󱊽

Level up by running

No grinding. Kilometres are the only way to level up. 5 km gets you to level 2, and 250 km to level 10.

󰨁

New places to open

You start at a tavern called The Rusty Flagon. As you level up, the road opens further out. What is out there is yours to find.

󰓜

Shops that stay put

Every place has a shop, and its list never reshuffles. A sword you can't afford today is there, at the same price, when you come back.

󰓥

Abilities and gear

Some levels teach you a new ability, and some make you choose between two. Weapons and armour come from shops, fights and the road.

󰁨

Never the same story

An AI writes every scene around where you are and what you carry. No two runs tell the same story, and your choices change what comes next.

The dice are real.

Fights are rolled on your phone. The AI doesn't decide if you hit. d20 + Strength + proficiency against the enemy's armour, weapon dice for damage, doubled on a natural 20.

The narrator only comes back once it's over, to tell you how it went.

Lose, and the story stops. The run doesn't. Your kilometres still count, and you're back on your feet for the next one.

07Questions

Common questions.

Do I have to look at my phone?

Only to make a choice. The story plays in your earbuds. When a scene ends you look down, tap one of four big buttons, and keep going.

Do I need to know Dungeons & Dragons?

No. The dice are rolled for you and the maths is done by the app. If you do know D&D, you will recognise the rules: d20 to hit, armour class, strength and dexterity.

Does it work on a treadmill?

No. RuneRun uses GPS to place runes on real streets and to measure your distance, so you need to be outside.

What happens if my character dies?

The story stops, but your run does not. You keep running and your distance still counts toward your level. When you end the run, your character comes back with some health.

Which phones does it run on?

RuneRun is coming to Android first.

What counts as one of my four free runs?

Any run you start and save. The count resets on the first of each month.
