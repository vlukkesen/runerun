Privacy policy

What leaves your phone

RuneRun has no accounts. Your character, your runs, your routes and your settings are stored on your phone and nowhere else.

Last updated 23 September 2026.

What the app sends, and why

To play a run, RuneRun has to send a few things to other services. None of it includes your name, email address, phone number or any ID that points to you.

Your approximate area, to OpenStreetMap. When a run starts, the app asks public OpenStreetMap servers for the streets in a box of about 2 km around you, so it can put runes on real roads. Your exact GPS position is never sent. It is used on the phone to measure distance and pace.

The scene and your choice, to the story and voice services. When you tap a rune or a choice, the app sends the current scene, your character sheet and the option you tapped to Anthropic, which writes the next part of the story, and the story text to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, which reads it aloud. The places in the story are made up. Your real location is not included.

We don't keep any of this. The services above handle the request and send the answer back. Their own privacy policies apply to how they process it: OpenStreetMap, Anthropic, Google.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, no tracking and no advertising ID.
  • No ads.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No location tracking in the background. GPS is only used while a run is on the screen.

Purchases

RuneRun Plus is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment. We only learn whether your subscription is active, not your payment details.

Permissions

  • Location, to place runes and measure your run.
  • Internet, for the map, the story and the narration.
  • Vibration, for haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.

Deleting your data

Everything RuneRun keeps is on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of it. There is nothing on our side to delete.

Children

RuneRun is made for players aged 16 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
